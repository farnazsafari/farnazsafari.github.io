Farnaz Safari

Ph.D. Candidate · Charles H. Dyson School of Applied Economics and Management · Cornell University

I study labor market decisions and health in low- and middle-income countries.

My Job Market Paper is a field study in India on economic participation among working-age adults. It uses paid remote work to study labor supply and productivity.

In related work, I use high-frequency household panel data from Malawi to study occupational mobility, income diversification, and migration strategies.

Before my Ph.D., I completed an M.Sc. in Agricultural and Resource Economics at UC Davis.
