Part-time gig work on WhatsApp

Short voice recordings, handwriting and typing, all in Hindi. You start with a paid 15-minute trial, to see if you like the job. No experience is needed.

This work is by invitation. If you were invited, you start from the message you received on WhatsApp.
There is no fee, no deposit and nothing to buy.

Watch first

Three short videos, about one to one and a half minutes each, with sound.

The work

You get a photo of handwritten Hindi. For each photo there are three steps.

Voice

Read it aloud

You read the photo aloud and send it as a voice note.

Handwriting

Write it by hand

You write it on paper, in Hindi letters, and send a photo of your page.

Typing

Type it in Roman letters

You type what the photo says in Roman letters, the way you type on WhatsApp. Then the next photo comes.

This is a common kind of gig work in India: companies pay people to read, write and type in Hindi, for example to teach AI models the language. In this project, your work is used for research, not to train AI models.

How it works

Three steps. The first two are on WhatsApp.

  1. A paid trial of 15 minutes

    For you to see if you like the job. Before it, you tell us the lowest pay you would work for. How this decides your pay is explained below.

  2. A paid assessment of 1 hour

    The same work, for one hour. After the trial you choose whether to go on to it.

  3. Work days at a centre

    For a small number of people, at a public work centre in your city. Described below.

What you can earn

  • A fixed Rs 100 for answering questions, whether or not there is work for you
  • Your pay for the 15-minute trial
  • Your pay for the 1-hour assessment
  • A bonus of Rs 5 for every photo typed correctly

Everything is paid in one UPI transfer, 2 to 3 days after the work.

Work days at a centre

After the assessment come work days at a public work centre in your city. Places at the centre are limited this round, and not everyone gets a place.

The day

Four hours of work, 10:30 to 2:30, with lunch in between. Lunch is provided. The centre is open all seven days and you pick your day.

Getting there

A shared cab takes you from your nearest bus or railway station at 10 am and brings you back. Your public transport fare is reimbursed. We never ask for your home address.

Your co-worker

You work in a pair with one co-worker of your own gender. One of you reads Hindi aloud, and the other types it and writes it by hand. Then you swap, and you check each other's work. You share the ride and lunch.

Your certificate

After the day you get a certificate of completing the job. You choose whether it shows your first name only or your full name.

How pay is set

When you buy onions, you have a price in mind. If the seller asks that price or less, you buy. If the seller asks more, you walk on.

The employer does the same with this work. The employer has a fixed pay in mind for the job. You do not know this amount. There is no bargaining.

So you tell us the lowest pay you would work for. Below it, you would rather not spend your time on this job.

  • If your number is equal to or lower than the employer's pay, you are hired, and you are paid the employer's pay, which is equal to or above the pay you asked for.
  • If your number is above the employer's pay, you are not hired for that job. Your Rs 100 for answering the questions is still yours, even if you do not get the job.
  • A higher number does not give more pay.
  • A number lower than your real one can mean working for less than the job is worth to you.

So the most truthful answer is your best answer.

Your privacy

Your voice recordings, handwriting, typing and answers are used for research. They are kept confidential and stored without your name.

  • Your voice recordings, photos of your handwriting, typing and answers are stored under a study ID, not under your name.
  • Your name, phone number and UPI ID are stored separately from your work and answers, on encrypted servers.
  • Your name never appears in any published results.
  • Data may be shared with the research community only after any personal information that could identify you is removed.
  • The review boards and the funder named in the consent form may check anonymous records. They do not receive your name, phone number or other identifying information.
  • We never ask for your home address.
  • We do our best to keep your participation confidential, but we cannot guarantee complete confidentiality.

Taking part is your choice. You can stop at any time and keep what you have earned.

How to know it is us

  • We never ask you for a fee, a deposit or a purchase.
  • We never ask for your UPI PIN, an OTP, a card number or a password.
  • If a message asks you for money in our name, it is not from us. You can report it to the cybercrime helpline, 1930.

Common questions

What kind of work will I do?

You get photos of handwritten Hindi. You read each one aloud as a voice note, write it by hand on paper and send a photo of your page, then type it in Roman letters. You see an example first.

Do I need experience or a computer?

No experience is needed. The trial is done on your phone, in WhatsApp. You need a pen and paper.

Who is this for?

This round is by invitation only. People who are invited get a message on WhatsApp and start from there. There is no sign-up on this page.

What kind of position is this?

Part-time work, paid for each piece of work you do. There is no long commitment.

Will everyone get a work day at the centre?

No. Places at the centre are limited this round, and not everyone gets a place.

What is the assessment?

One hour of the same work on your phone. It is paid work. After the trial you choose whether to go on to it. Work days at the centre come after it.

What is my work used for?

For research. This kind of work is common gig work in India, and companies often pay for it to teach AI models Hindi. In this project, your work is not used to train AI models.

When am I paid?

Everything is paid in one UPI transfer, 2 to 3 days after the work.

Where is the full consent form?

In the "Who runs this" section of the Hindi page, as a PDF in Hindi.

Who sees my answers and recordings?

Your work and answers are stored under a study ID, not under your name. Your name, phone number and UPI ID are kept separately. The details are in the "Your privacy" section of this page.
